Our Story

A little more gold in every moment.

Jhankaar brings together contemporary design and timeless Indian jewellery aesthetics, creating pieces that make everyday moments feel a little more special — worn to work on a Tuesday, or to a wedding in December.

It began with a simple frustration: jewellery that looked like gold rarely felt like it belonged to real life — too precious to wear, too expensive to enjoy. Jhankaar was built to close that gap, one piece at a time.

Every design starts as a sketch before it becomes a mould, and every mould is checked by hand before it reaches you. We're a small team working out of Telangana, and we still pack every order ourselves.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our anti-tarnish jewellery, care, sizing, and delivery. Can't find your answer below — just WhatsApp us.

What exactly is "anti-tarnish" jewellery?

Anti-tarnish jewellery is made by electroplating a fine layer of real gold over a base metal — usually brass or an alloy — using a protective coating that resists fading, discolouration, and tarnishing far longer than standard gold plating. You get the same designs, detailing, and shine as traditional gold jewellery, with everyday durability built in, which is why it's become the go-to choice for both daily wear and special occasions.

Will the gold plating fade, chip, or tarnish?

Our anti-tarnish coating is built to resist fading and discolouration far better than standard gold plating, and holds its shine for years with reasonable care. It isn't indestructible, though — direct contact with water, perfume, deodorant, sweat, and harsh chemicals will still wear it down faster over time. A simple rule: put your jewellery on last, after makeup and perfume, and take it off first before you shower, swim, or work out.

Is it safe for sensitive skin?

Most of our customers wear these pieces daily without any issue. If you know you react to certain metals, we'd suggest a short patch test before wearing a new piece for an extended period, and avoiding prolonged contact with broken or irritated skin. If you have specific concerns, message us on WhatsApp before you order and we'll talk you through the materials used.

How should I clean and store my jewellery?

Wipe each piece gently with a soft, dry cloth after wearing it — avoid water, soap, and jewellery cleaning liquids, which can strip the plating. Store items separately in the pouch or box they arrived in, away from direct sunlight and humidity, so they don't scratch against each other or discolour over time.

What's your exchange and return policy?

We accept exchanges on unused pieces, with tags and original packaging intact, within 7 days of delivery — message us on WhatsApp with your order details and we'll guide you through it. Customised or made-to-order pieces aren't eligible for exchange. We currently offer exchange for another item of equal or greater value rather than a cash refund.

Do you ship across India, and how long does delivery take?

Yes, we ship pan-India. Orders are usually packed and dispatched within 2–3 business days, with delivery taking another 3–7 business days depending on your location. You'll receive tracking details over WhatsApp as soon as your order is on its way.

Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)?

COD is available on select pin codes for orders under a certain value, and the option will show at checkout if it's available for your address. For higher-value and bridal orders, we request prepayment or a partial advance, which we'll confirm with you over WhatsApp before your order is packed.

Can I request a custom design or place a bulk/wedding order?

Absolutely — this is something we do often. Share a reference photo or describe what you have in mind over WhatsApp, and we'll let you know what's possible, the approximate cost, and the timeline. For weddings and bulk orders, we recommend reaching out at least 3–4 weeks in advance so we have enough time to source and craft everything properly.

How do I find my correct ring or bangle size?

If you already have a ring or bangle that fits well, measure its inner diameter and send us the number over WhatsApp — we'll match it to the closest size we stock. Ordering as a gift and can't get a size? Go slightly larger — a ring is far easier to resize down than a bangle.

    1. Who we are

    Jhankaar ("we", "us", "our") is an anti-tarnish jewellery brand operating from Agora Space, near Shri Shikshayatan School, Kolkata, West Bengal, India. This policy applies to everyone who visits our website, browses our catalogue, or contacts us — whether or not you go on to make a purchase.

    We are a small, WhatsApp-first business: we don't run a full online checkout or store payment details on our servers. Understanding that is helpful context for the rest of this policy.

    2. Information we collect

    We only collect information that you choose to give us, or that's generated automatically as part of using the website. We do not require you to create an account to browse or shop.

    Information you give us directly

    • Enquiries and orders: When you message us via WhatsApp — whether by tapping "Enquire," "Order via WhatsApp," or using our contact form — we receive whatever information you choose to share in that conversation, typically your name, phone number, delivery address, and details of the product you're interested in.
    • Contact form: Our on-site contact form doesn't submit to a server we control. It simply opens WhatsApp with your name and message pre-filled, so that conversation happens directly between you and our WhatsApp Business number.

    Information collected automatically

    • Cart and wishlist: The items you add to your cart or wishlist are saved in your own browser's local storage, not on our servers. We never see this list unless you choose to send it to us (for example, by tapping "Order via WhatsApp," which puts the item list into a WhatsApp message you control before sending).
    • Basic technical data: Like most websites, our hosting provider and any content-delivery or font services we use may log standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, for security and performance purposes.

    We do not collect payment card details, government ID numbers, or any sensitive personal data through this website.

    3. Cookies & local storage

    We keep our use of browser storage minimal and functional:

    • Local storage is used to remember your cart and wishlist between visits, entirely on your own device.
    • Session storage is used only to keep our store administrator signed in to the site's admin panel during an active session — this has nothing to do with regular visitors.
    • We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't run analytics scripts that build a profile of you across other websites.

    Embedded third-party content on our site — such as Google Maps and Google Fonts — may set their own cookies or make their own requests, governed by their respective privacy policies (linked below). You can control or clear cookies at any time through your browser settings.

    4. How we use your information

    We use the information shared with us to:

    • Respond to your enquiries and process orders you place with us over WhatsApp;
    • Coordinate delivery, exchanges, and after-sales support;
    • Share order updates, tracking details, or requested product information;
    • Improve our catalogue, website content, and customer experience;
    • Meet legal, accounting, or regulatory requirements where applicable.

    We do not use your information for automated decision-making, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties, ever.

    5. Third-party services we use

    To run this website, we rely on a small number of trusted service providers, each of which processes limited data under its own privacy policy:

    • WhatsApp / Meta: All enquiries, orders, and customer conversations happen over WhatsApp Business. Meta processes those messages under its own WhatsApp Privacy Policy.
    • Google Fonts: We use Google Fonts to display typography on this site, which may involve your browser requesting font files from Google's servers.
    • Google Maps: Our "Visit Us" section embeds a Google Map to help you find our store, subject to Google's own privacy practices.
    • Supabase (database hosting): Our product catalogue, collections, and site content are stored using Supabase, a database platform. This stores the products and content we publish — it is not used to collect or store customer personal information, and public visitors only have read access to this catalogue data.

    Note: If we add an online payment gateway, delivery-tracking integration, or customer account system in the future, we'll update this policy to reflect exactly what data that service collects and how it's used.

    6. Sharing & disclosure

    We don't sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share it in these limited situations:

    • With delivery and logistics partners, solely to fulfil and ship your order;
    • With the service providers listed above, to the extent needed to operate our website and catalogue;
    • If required by law, regulation, legal process, or a valid governmental request;
    • To protect the rights, property, or safety of Jhankaar, our customers, or others, where legally permitted.

    7. Orders, payments & Cash on Delivery

    We do not process or store payment card details through this website. Orders are confirmed directly with you over WhatsApp, where we'll share available payment options — which may include prepayment, a partial advance for bridal or bulk orders, or Cash on Delivery (COD) where available. Any payment you make is handled through the method agreed in that direct conversation, not through this website.

    8. Data retention

    We keep conversation and order information for as long as reasonably needed to fulfil your order, handle any exchanges or after-sales queries, and meet our accounting or legal obligations — after which it is deleted or anonymised where feasible. Cart and wishlist data stored in your browser remains there until you clear it yourself or clear your browser storage.

    9. How we protect your data

    We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we hold — including restricting access to our admin panel and catalogue database to authenticated store administrators only. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

    • Access the personal information we hold about you;
    • Correct any information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
    • Delete your information from our records, subject to any legal obligation we may have to retain it;
